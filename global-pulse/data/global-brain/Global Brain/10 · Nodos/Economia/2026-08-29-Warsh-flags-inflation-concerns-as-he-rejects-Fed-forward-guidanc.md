# Warsh flags inflation concerns as he rejects Fed forward guidance

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Economia]] · Impacto 83

## Sintesis
Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh used his first Jackson Hole address on Friday to argue that financial conditions are not restrictive and that underlying inflation has not meaningfully improved, a hawkish assessment that strengthens the case for a rate hike, while ruling out forward guidance.
