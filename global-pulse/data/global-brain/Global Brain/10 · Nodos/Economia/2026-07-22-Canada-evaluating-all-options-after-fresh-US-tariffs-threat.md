# Canada evaluating 'all options' after fresh US tariffs threat

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Economia]] · Impacto 83

## Sintesis
Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney said Tuesday he was looking at "all options" after US President Donald Trump made plans to impose new 50 percent tariffs on many Canadian goods in 30 days. Carney also said that he and Trump had agreed to "intensify discussions" in the coming weeks on a possible deal, but did not suggest he would travel to Washington for any talks. FRANCE 24's Christopher Guly reports from Ottawa.
