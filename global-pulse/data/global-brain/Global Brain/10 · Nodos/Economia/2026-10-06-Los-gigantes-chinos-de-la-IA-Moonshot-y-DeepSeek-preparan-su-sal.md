# Los gigantes chinos de la IA Moonshot y DeepSeek preparan su salida a Bolsa tras disparar su valoración

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Economia]] · Impacto 69

## Sintesis
Moonshot AI, start-up china de IA, planea cotizar en Hong Kong en 2027 tras valer 50.000 millones de dólares, mientras DeepSeek capta hasta 100.000 millones de yuanes.

## Synthesis (EN)
Chinese AI start-up Moonshot AI is targeting for a Hong Kong listing in early 2027 after its valuation hit $50 billion (€44.6bn), while rival DeepSeek is raising between ¥80 billion (€10.6bn) and ¥100 billion (€13.3bn) in a round led by Tencent and CATL, fuelling bets on its own market debut.
