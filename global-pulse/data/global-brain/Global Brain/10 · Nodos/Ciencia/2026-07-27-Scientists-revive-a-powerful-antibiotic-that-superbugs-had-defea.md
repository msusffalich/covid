# Scientists revive a powerful antibiotic that superbugs had defeated

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Ciencia]] · Impacto 75

## Sintesis
Scientists have found a way to revive vancomycin, a powerful antibiotic that some dangerous bacteria have learned to resist. Instead of creating an entirely new drug, researchers paired vancomycin with a small molecule called pghi-4, which blocks a bacterial enzyme linked to resistance. The combination restored the antibiotic’s ability to kill drug-resistant E.
