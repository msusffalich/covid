# Indonesia: potente erupción del Anak Krakatoa provoca cancelación de vuelos y capa intensa de ceniza

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Tecnologia]] · Impacto 95

## Sintesis
Una potente erupción del volcán Anak Krakatoa en Indonesia obligó a la cancelación de cientos de vuelos. La columna de ceniza alcanzó hasta 15 km de altura y afectó a miles de personas, quienes reportaron intensas capas de ceniza. Mientras tanto, las autoridades mantienen la alerta.

## Synthesis (EN)
Ash cloud from Anak Krakatau affects hundreds of flights and some school and fishing activities Indonesia halted operations at eight airports on Sunday, including from its main international hub, affecting hundreds of flights as a result of an ash cloud from the Anak Krakatau volcano. Saturday’s eruption, which produced lava and booming sounds that were heard up to 435 miles (700km) away, according to Indonesia’s geology agency, also interrupted some school activities and fishing. Two more eruptions were recorded on Sunday.
