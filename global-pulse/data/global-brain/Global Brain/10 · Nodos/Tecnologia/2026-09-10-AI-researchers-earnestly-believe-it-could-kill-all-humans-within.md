# AI researchers 'earnestly believe' it could kill all humans within the next decade, Anthropic researcher says

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Tecnologia]] · Impacto 60

## Sintesis
There is a more than 10 per cent chance that AI could kill all humans within the next decade, a researcher at Anthropic estimated in a post, hours after social media posts went viral from someone saying they had just quit Anthropic over concerns that AI companies are “gambling with our lives” in the AI development race.
