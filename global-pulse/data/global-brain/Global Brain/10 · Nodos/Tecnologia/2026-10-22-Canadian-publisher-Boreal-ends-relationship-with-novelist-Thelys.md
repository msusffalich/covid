# Canadian publisher Boréal ends relationship with novelist Thélyson Orélien over alleged use of AI

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Tecnologia]] · Impacto 66

## Sintesis
Author withdrawn from France’s Prix Goncourt last month has repeatedly denied using artificial intelligence to write novel The publisher of top-selling Haitian Canadian writer Thélyson Orélien, who was removed from France’s most prestigious literary prize list last month, has confirmed the author used artificial intelligence to help write his novel C’était ça ou mourir (It Was Either That or Die). Orélien has denied allegations that he used AI on his breakthrough novel and, last week, Canadian publisher Les Éditions du Boréal said it was standing by the 38-year-old writer. Continue reading...
