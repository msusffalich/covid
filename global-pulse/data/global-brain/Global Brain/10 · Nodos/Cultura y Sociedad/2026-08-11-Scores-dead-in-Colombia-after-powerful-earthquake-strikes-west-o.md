# Scores dead in Colombia after powerful earthquake strikes west of country

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Cultura y Sociedad]] · Impacto 60

## Sintesis
Quake of 7.4 magnitude collapses and severely damages buildings in several major cities, including Cali and Pereira More than 100 people died after a powerful earthquake struck western Colombia, collapsing and severely damaging scores of buildings in major cities including Cali and Pereira. The US and Colombian geological surveys reported that a 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck at 7.34am local time on Monday, with its epicentre in the western department of Chocó. It was felt in neighbouring Venezuela, still reeling from a deadly double earthquake on 24 June.
