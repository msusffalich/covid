# El rial iraní toca un mínimo histórico mientras Trump amenaza con una "guerra económica"

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 95

## Sintesis
Mientras Estados Unidos amenaza con nuevas sanciones contra Irán, el rial iraní cayó a un mínimo histórico frente al dólar, según medios locales. Las autoridades iraníes relativizaron la amenaza de Donald Trump, quien dijo que libraría una "guerra económica" contra Teherán. Los residentes en la capital tienen opiniones encontradas sobre cómo debería actuar el Gobierno.

## Synthesis (EN)
Collapse of trade talks could lead to job losses, but biggest impact on traditional allies is expected to be political The US has imposed 50% tariffs on some goods from Canada, and Mark Carney, the country’s prime minister, has vowed to match them “dollar for dollar” after the collapse of trade talks. The tariffs came into force on roughly $20bn (£14.6bn) of goods – ranging from hockey sticks to tongue depressors – at 04.00GMT. Trade experts said the tariffs could result in some job losses, but the largest impact is expected to be political, driving a further wedge between the traditional allies.
