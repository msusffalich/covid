## Sintesis
El empresario Steve Witkoff y el yerno de Trump, Jared Kushner, llegaron a Moscú y viajarán el domingo a Kiev con el objetivo de reactivar las negociaciones de paz

## Synthesis (EN)
‘Substantive plans’ will be announced in ‘coming weeks’ as envoys head to Kyiv for talks with Ukraine’s president, Volodymyr Zelenskyy Donald Trump’s envoys “discussed substantive plans for next steps” for ending the war between Ukraine and Russia during their meeting on Saturday in Moscow with Vladimir Putin, the White House said. Those steps would be announced in “coming weeks”, a White House official said in a statement, and the envoys “look forward to equally productive meetings tomorrow [Sunday] in Ukraine”.
