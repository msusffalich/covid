# Guterres condena el ataque de los hutíes contra el aeropuerto de Riad, Arabia Saudita

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 95

## Sintesis
Un ataque con misiles de los hutíes contra el principal aeropuerto de Riad dejó al menos 12 muertos y más de 300 heridos. El Secretario General de la ONU condenó el bombardeo y exigió el cese inmediato de los ataques contra civiles, en medio de una nueva escalada de la guerra en Yemen que agrava las tensiones regionales.

## Synthesis (EN)
US president’s plan to park the wars in Iran and Yemen, while blaming Ukraine for diesel prices, looks unsustainable The reckless missile attack on King Khalid international airport in Riyadh on Saturday did not just export the death and misery inside Yemen to Saudi Arabia, it also left Donald Trump forced to reconsider his diplomatic and political strategy designed to take him into the midterm elections next month. The US president’s plan had only been unveiled on Friday. He said it was the result of “a world problem” – spiralling diesel prices – which he blamed on Volodymyr Zelenskyy’s attacks on Russian oil refineries.
