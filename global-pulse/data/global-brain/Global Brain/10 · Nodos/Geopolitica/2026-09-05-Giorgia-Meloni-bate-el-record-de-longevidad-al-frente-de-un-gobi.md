# Giorgia Meloni bate el récord de longevidad al frente de un gobierno italiano

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 91

## Sintesis
A partir de este viernes, el gobierno de Giorgia Meloni es el más longevo de los 80 años de historia de la República italiana: 1.413 días, es decir, casi cuatro años. Sobre las claves de este hito, el informe de nuestro corresponsal en Roma, Federico Guiglia.

## Synthesis (EN)
Alison Sargent is pleased to welcome Political analyst Lorenzo Pregliasco, co-founder of polling agency YouTrend and adjunct professor at the University of Bologna. He argues that Giorgia Meloni’s unusual longevity in office is best understood not simply as a record of political strength, but as the product of a carefully calibrated governing strategy. Meloni has maintained stability partly by avoiding structural reforms disruptive enough to mobilize multiple constituencies against her at once.
