# Russia pounds Kyiv for five hours in one of its biggest ballistic missile attacks

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 83

## Sintesis
At least one person killed after dozens of missiles arrive in less than an hour and three-storey building catches fire Russia has carried out one of its biggest-ever ballistic missile attacks on Kyiv, launching a five-hour raid that left at least one person dead and seven injured, with fires and damage across the city. Ukrainian officials said the capital was hit with about 40 Iskander-M and hypersonic Zircon missiles. Residents heard an air raid siren sound at 1.30am.
