# Trump anuncia planes de inversión energética surcoreana por 200.000 millones de dólares

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 87

## Sintesis
Trump anunció inversiones surcoreanas por 200.000 millones de dólares en energía de EEUU, incluidos 54.000 millones para un proyecto de GNL en Alaska que Seúl sostiene que aún no ha aceptado financiar.

## Synthesis (EN)
Support such as for white Afrikaners and anti-communism reveals how US is redefining what qualifies as human rights With hours left before the funding expires, the Trump administration is pushing to direct more than $175m of the state department’s flagship human rights fund to a slate of awards that includes a huge $40m grant for an anti-communism fund, support for a group advancing white Afrikaner rights in South Africa, a campaign against Brazil’s supreme court and programs that would reward ideological allies in Europe who protect “western civilizational norms”. On Wednesday, the state department obligated the funds to the grantees hours before the deadline despite congressional concerns and requests not to do so, according to a source familiar with the negotiations. The state department declined to comment on questions from the Guardian specifically on whether it had obligated the funds and had “blown” holds from Congress due to concerns about the money’s use.
