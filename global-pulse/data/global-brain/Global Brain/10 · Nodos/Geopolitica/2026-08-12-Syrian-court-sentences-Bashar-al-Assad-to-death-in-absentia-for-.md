> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 83

## Sintesis
Verdict relates to atrocities committed during the country’s 14-year civil war in which about 500,000 people died A Syrian court has sentenced the country’s former president Bashar al-Assad and his brother Maher to death in absentia for crimes against humanity and war crimes during the 14-year civil war, in which about half a million people died.
