# US Space Force confirms weapons deployed in space for first time

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K2 · Estelar y Energia]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 78

## Sintesis
The US Space Force on Monday confirmed for the first time that Washington has weapons deployed in orbit, as the United States, China and Russia expand their military capabilities in space. The disclosure highlights a growing focus on controlling the space domain and protecting satellites vital to military communications, surveillance and navigation.
