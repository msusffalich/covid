# Trump retira a Siria de la lista de "patrocinadores del terrorismo" tras 47 años: ¿qué significa?

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 87

## Sintesis
Tras cumplirse los 45 días formales de plazo desde la notificación al Congreso, el Gobierno de Donald Trump quitó oficialmente a Siria de su lista de países "patrocinadores del terrorismo", en la que se encontraba desde 1979. Este paso significa el levantamiento de una serie de sanciones, que Damasco consideraba el "último obstáculo" para permitir que la reconexión del país con el sistema financiero y económico global.

## Synthesis (EN)
The state department will revoke B1 and B2 visas issued between 2016 and 2026, whose holders have sought or are now seeking asylum Donald Trump’s administration said on Monday it planned to revoke the non-immigrant visas of foreigners who have applied for or are currently seeking asylum status in the US, marking its latest step in a sweeping immigration crackdown. A state department spokesperson said it was coordinating with the Department of Homeland Security “to identify and revoke the non-immigrant visas of foreigners who have come to the United States claiming to be short-term visitors, but then file for asylum to stay here permanently”.
