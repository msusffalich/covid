# Qué es la montaña Pickaxe, la instalación secreta y fuertemente protegida de Irán que Trump ha prometido atacar "con dureza"

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Geopolitica]] · Impacto 95

## Sintesis
Preguntado sobre los informes que indican que Teherán podría haber trasladado centrifugadoras nucleares a la montaña Pickaxe, el presidente Donald Trump prometió que Estados Unidos atacaría el complejo subterráneo "con mucha fuerza... probablemente muy pronto".

## Synthesis (EN)
President flew to Ankara onboard Qatari-gifted jet, without advanced defenses, and flew home on old Air Force One Donald Trump switched aircraft before departing Turkey earlier this month after US officials determined there was a credible threat from Iranian proxy forces targeting him and Air Force One, according to a new report from the New York Times . The threat surfaced while the US president was attending the Nato summit. He had arrived in Ankara onboard the new Qatari-donated Boeing 747-8, an aircraft that Trump acknowledged does not have the same advanced defensive systems as the older Air Force One.
