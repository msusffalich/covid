# RFK Jr promises anti-vaxxers a ‘friend at the White House’ amid deadly measles outbreak

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K0 · Local y Nacional]] · Categoria: [[Salud y Bienestar]] · Impacto 66

## Sintesis
The US health secretary added that he would’ve made sweeping changes ‘on day one’ but he had to follow the law As the death toll from the worst measles outbreak in decades rises, Robert F Kennedy Jr headlined the Washington DC conference of the anti-vaccine organization Children’s Health Defense, which he led for nearly a decade. “Let me be clear. You have a strong and steadfast friend at the White House,” Kennedy, now serving as secretary of the US Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), told anti-vaccine activists at the conference in a nearly hour-and-a-half-long speech.
