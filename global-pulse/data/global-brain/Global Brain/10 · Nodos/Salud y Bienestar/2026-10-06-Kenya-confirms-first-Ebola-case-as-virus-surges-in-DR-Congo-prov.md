# Kenya confirms first Ebola case as virus ‘surges’ in DR Congo province

> [!info] Escala Kardashev: [[K1 · Planetario]] · Categoria: [[Salud y Bienestar]] · Impacto 83

## Sintesis
Health minister says 28 contacts identified after death of citizen who arrived from DRC on Saturday Kenya has reported its first-ever Ebola death, the health minister announced on Tuesday, after a Kenyan man who had been living in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) died after returning to the country. In mid-May, the DRC declared its 17th Ebola outbreak, an extremely deadly disease that has killed more than 15,000 people in Africa over the past 50 years. Continue reading...
